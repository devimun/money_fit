MoneyFit helps you set a daily or monthly discretionary budget, see what you can spend today, and understand your spending flow. Record essential and discretionary expenses separately, then review them in the calendar and statistics.

Set your daily or monthly discretionary spending budget and instantly see how much you can freely spend right now.

"How much can I spend today without worry?"
MoneyFit answers this question with a clear, precise number to support everyday budget decisions.

The key to financial wellness is managing your discretionary spending. When you know exactly how much you can freely spend after fixed expenses, spending becomes a moment of freedom rather than guilt.

Key Features

Daily & Monthly Spending Limits
Set your discretionary spending limit by day or month, and track your remaining balance in real time.

Intuitive Spending Visualization
See your daily and monthly expenses and remaining budget at a glance with pie charts.

Calendar-Based Spending Analysis
View your spending flow throughout the month on a calendar and discover your spending patterns.

Achievement Tracking & Motivation
Track consecutive days of staying within your budget and celebrate your progress.

Spending Analysis
Compare discretionary and essential spending and view your top three spending categories.

Customizable Expense Management
Filter expenses by category or time period.

Personalized Themes
Change the app's look with a selection of themes.
